A sixteen-second festive product film for a haircare brand, set inside a warm peach studio dreamworld of glowing shelves, slow conveyor belts and stacked gifts. Six shots joined by hard cuts. The only objects are the products, their gift boxes, ornaments and gift bags.

ACTIVE REFERENCES

One continuous world in warm peach. Set surfaces are soft peach plaster and matte peach velvet, conveyor slats are warm cream, rails and tubes are clear glass catching highlights. Every shot has a near layer, the products in the middle layer, and soft peach walls falling off into gentle haze at 3 to 4 metres. Light comes from the camera-left side in every shot.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Frame one is already full: a wall of glowing shelf cubbies with products standing in them, the camera centred and level. Every object is already in motion from frame one, exactly as in @video1.

FORMAT MODE
Timed multishot, six segments. Cuts only at the specified points, the camera does not cut on its own.

OPTICS
Rectilinear lens, crisp product focus, soft natural falloff behind. Segment FOV: 47° for shots 1 to 3, 29° for shots 4 to 6, no drift mid-segment. Framing in each segment matches @video1.

CAMERA
The camera follows @video1 exactly: same height, distance, direction and speed of every move. Moves are smooth and eased in and out, with a calm settle at the end of each one. Tonal character: soft highlight roll-off, creamy whites, deep warm shadows with detail.

SCENE CONTEXT
At sunrise on a snow ridge 2,600 meters high above a sea of clouds, a woman in a long crimson wool coat raises her gloved left arm, and a golden eagle flies straight out of the rising sun and lands on her fist. Four shots joined by three HARD CUTS, 20 seconds total.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@image1 — storyboard page 1 (shots 1 and 2): camera position, framing and screen direction only; every look comes from this text.
@image2 — storyboard page 2 (shots 3 and 4): camera position, framing and screen direction only; every look comes from this text.
In the panels the red cylinder is the woman, the brown shape is the eagle and the sphere is the sun; grey shapes are placeholders for blocking.

WOMAN: about 30, slim and strong, dark hair in one thick braid down her back, wind-flushed cheeks, a long deep-crimson wool coat to the shins, a thick tan leather falconry gauntlet on her left forearm up to the elbow, dark leather boots. She is the only person on the mountain.
EAGLE: an adult golden eagle, dark brown plumage, golden-bronze feathers on the nape, hooked dark beak with a yellow base, yellow feet, black talons; its wingspan 2.2 meters, wider than the woman is tall.

LOCATION MAP
A narrow snow crest runs west to east with a wind-carved cornice; the cloud sea lies 30 meters below on both sides and stretches to the horizon.

COMPASS (fixed backdrop)
E: the sun disk rising out of the cloud sea, gold light spilling across the cloud tops. W: a dark limestone peak in blue shadow, snow lining its gullies. S: jagged grey peaks rising out of the clouds, lit on their east faces. N: a snow spur falling away into the clouds.

GEOGRAPHY
The woman stands on the crest facing east, toward the sun, her gloved left arm raised toward the north-east. The eagle flies out of the sun toward her, east to west, and lands on her left fist. Every camera stays on the north side of the crest.

FORMAT MODE
Timed multishot, 20 seconds. Cuts only at the stated seconds; the camera does not cut on its own. FOV fixed per shot, no drift mid-segment.

0.0s to 5.0s — SHOT 1: THE RIDGE (CAM FACING: S) (PANELS 1A, 1B)
Size: extreme wide. Lens: 63°, rectilinear. Mount: a drone rising straight up out of the cloud sea 35 meters north of the crest at 12 km/h, ending 1.5 meters above crest level with a slight forward drift. The shot opens inside the glowing cloud tops, vapor streaming past the lens; the camera breaks through and the crest rises into frame as a bright white line across the middle of the frame. The woman stands at frame centre on the crest, a small crimson figure facing frame left toward the sun, the southern peaks rising behind her. The sun sits just off frame left and rakes warm light across the snow from frame left. As the camera levels with her, she slowly raises her gloved left arm from her side to shoulder height, fist toward frame left.

5.0s HARD CUT

5.0s to 9.0s — SHOT 2: OUT OF THE SUN (CAM FACING: E) (PANELS 2A, 2B)
Size: long-lens wide. Lens: 18° telephoto, strong compression. Mount: handheld 6 meters behind her at 1.8 meters height, a 1–2 cm tremor, otherwise steady. The sun disk sits at frame centre on the cloud horizon, huge through the compression. Her back is a dark silhouette in the lower right third, the crimson coat edged in a thin gold rim, her gloved arm raised toward frame left of centre; a southern peak silhouette rises lower centre. A black speck appears inside the sun disk and grows into the eagle: deep slow wingbeats, then wings locked into a glide straight toward the lens at 60 km/h. Heat shimmer and haze hang in a compressed wall of gold between the camera and the sun. Focus on the eagle; she stays soft.

9.0s HARD CUT

9.0s to 15.0s — SHOT 3: THE LANDING (CAM FACING: SE, tilted 35° up) (PANELS 3A, 3B, 3C)
The whole shot plays in slow motion at 25% speed. Size: low-angle wide. Lens: 84° wide, rectilinear. Mount: static, 0.3 meters above the snow at her feet. Her crimson coat towers up the right side of frame, her gloved left fist out right of centre against the deep blue sky; open sky fills frame left. The eagle sweeps in from frame left, banks, swings its feet forward and flares its wings fully open, wider than she is tall, primary feathers splayed like fingers and flexing one by one in the air, the low sun striking through them from behind in gold edges. Its talons open and close around the leather gauntlet; her arm dips 10 centimeters under the impact and lifts back level. The downdraft blasts a sheet of powder snow off the crest across the lens, glittering in the backlight. Her braid and the coat hem whip sideways.

15.0s HARD CUT

15.0s to 20.0s — SHOT 4: TOGETHER (CAM FACING: W) (PANELS 4A, 4B)
Real time. Size: medium close-up two-shot. Lens: 29°, portrait compression. Mount: slow dolly push from 3.2 to 2.4 meters at 0.6 km/h, eye level 1.7 meters. Her face sits frame centre-left, warm sunrise light on it from behind the camera; the eagle stands upright on her left fist at frame right, its head level with hers; the dark limestone peak in blue shadow fills the soft background. The eagle folds its wings in two shrugs and ruffles its neck feathers, then turns its head and looks straight into the lens, gold eye bright, blinking once. She breathes out a cloud of visible breath; the corners of her eyes crease and a small smile lifts her cheeks while her gaze stays on the eagle.

PERFORMANCE
She is calm and certain: shoulders steady, slow breath, eyes locked on the eagle from the moment it appears. Real skin texture, wind-flushed cheeks, the sun as a catch-light in both eyes. The eagle moves with small, sharp, alert head turns.

PHYSICS
Real mass: the eagle weighs 5 kilograms; the landing loads her arm and she braces with a half-step back of her right foot. Each feather reacts to the air on its own. Powder snow falls back under gravity within 2 seconds. The heavy wool coat swings and settles with weight.

LIGHTING
Sunrise at 2,600 meters altitude. The sun sits 3 degrees above the cloud horizon due east, a warm 2800K key raking low and hard across the ridge, long shadows stretching west. The open sky fills from above in cool 9000K blue. Haze at 25 percent over the cloud sea, catching the sunlight in warm shafts 300 meters deep. The light stays low, warm-on-cool and hard-edged in every shot; shadows stay deep blue and keep their texture.

COLOR GRADE
60 percent cool blue in the sky and the snow in shadow, 30 percent warm gold on the cloud tops and the sunlit snow edges, 10 percent crimson reserved for her coat, the only red in every frame.

AUDIO
Wind across the crest, the heavy whoosh of the wingbeats, the creak of leather as the talons grip the gauntlet, her single exhale. No music.

STORYBOARD
Follow panels 1A to 4B in order, each a key frame: match camera position, framing, screen direction and action; cut only where the shot number changes. The panels are grey previz stand-ins; every look comes from this text.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action, natural-history documentary realism, crisp high-altitude air, fine organic film grain.

OUTPUT SETTINGS
16:9. Shots 1, 2 and 4 in real time; shot 3 in slow motion at 25% speed from start to finish.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The woman and the eagle are the only living beings in every shot. The eagle always travels east to west, out of the sun toward her. The gauntlet stays on her left arm. Her coat is the only red in frame. The sun stays low in the east in every shot. Every camera stays on the north side of the crest. Exactly three HARD CUTS, at 5.0s, 9.0s and 15.0s.

A chic, playful instrumental track for a 26-second luxury beauty and perfume reel. Tempo 118 BPM, 4/4, key of A minor moving to C major in the final section. Bright and confident, modern French-pop and nu-disco feel, polished and expensive, made for a fashion Instagram reel.
0 to 7 seconds: intro with a clean muted electric guitar riff, soft finger snaps on beats 2 and 4, a warm round bassline entering at 3 seconds, light shaker; airy, curious, rising.
7 to 15 seconds: the groove settles in: tight four-on-the-floor kick at low volume, funky clean guitar chops, glossy Rhodes chords, a short sparkling glockenspiel motif every two bars; relaxed, sensual, smiling.
15 to 24 seconds: energy lifts for a fast montage: open hi-hats on the off-beats, a playful plucked synth hook, string stabs on the downbeats, a quick rising riser into each bar; the most energetic part of the track.
24 to 26 seconds: a clean button ending: one final string stab and a single bright chime, then a short natural reverb tail to silence.
Fully instrumental, no vocals. Crisp, wide, clean mix with room for sound effects, no heavy bass drops.

# Dizajni i paneleve hologram — imazh reference (v1)
Modeli: Nano Banana Pro, 16:9, 4 variante. Pa referenca.
```
Design reference sheet of four futuristic holographic interface panels floating side by side in an empty deep navy-blue space, evenly spaced, each the proportion of a laptop screen, gently curved like a section of a cylinder, seen from the front with a slight angle.

Each panel is built from three layers of thin transparent glass stacked 2 centimetres apart, so the content floats at different depths with visible parallax. A crisp 1-pixel turquoise edge light runs around every layer, brighter at the four corners, where small L-shaped corner brackets glow. Fine horizontal scanlines shimmer across the glass at 10% opacity. Tiny turquoise light particles drift around each panel, and a soft cone of turquoise light rises from below into the bottom edge, as if the panel is projected upward from a device.

Panel 1: a vertical video frame with a glowing Instagram camera logo at its centre, a play triangle and a thin progress bar. Panel 2: a chat window with the green WhatsApp logo at the top and three rounded message bubbles stacked at different depths. Panel 3: an advertising dashboard with the blue Meta infinity logo, a rising bar chart and a curved line graph drawn in light. Panel 4: a large glowing shield with a check mark inside, surrounded by a slowly rotating ring of light segments.

All interface details are made of thin lines, dots, bars and simple shapes in turquoise and white light; the logos keep their own colours as small accents. The panels glow from within, casting a faint turquoise reflection on a dark glossy floor below them. Photoreal render, deep navy darkness for 60% of the frame, turquoise light for the panels, crisp detail, soft halo around every glowing edge.
```

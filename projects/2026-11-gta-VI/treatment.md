# THEY BROUGHT ME HERE: Director's Treatment (v1)

**Runtime:** ~63 s · **Format:** 9:16 · **Tone:** grounded crime drama. Real streets, real sweat, real weight.
Quiet before the storm, silence before the hit.
**Tone references (for us, never in prompts):** the night driving in *Drive* and *Collateral*, the restraint of *Heat*,
the warmth of *Boyhood* (one face growing up across time).

## The story in one breath
A boy who grew up inside these worlds is raised, era by era, by the men who drove them. Every era the police get
closer, and every era his heroes take a little more of the weight for him. On the last night, on a bridge with no
way out, the new generation comes to take him away. There is one seat. The old ones stay behind.

**Theme:** growing up means your heroes let you go.
**Emotional line:** wonder → courage → responsibility → loss → gratitude.

## Recurring motifs
| Motif | Function |
|---|---|
| **The rear-view mirror** | Every era opens on his eyes in the mirror. The eyes grow up: 10 → 15 → 22 → today. |
| **The red die** | Tommy gives it to him "for luck" in '86. It hangs in every car he rides in. Tommy throws it back to him at the end. |
| **A car turned sideways** | In '92 Tommy blocks the police with his car for the first time. The finale is the same move, made by everyone. |
| **Two fingers to the brow** | Tommy's salute in '92. The last image of Tommy at the end is the same salute. |
| **The seat** | Passenger → back seat → driver → leading the convoy → passenger again, now of the new generation. |

## COLD OPEN · "The Bridge" · 0:00–0:03
Night. Tight on a man's fist, knuckles white, red and blue light strobing across it. Through the fingers, a red die.
Pull up to his face, today's Egli, lit by a police spotlight, a wall of sirens around him. He closes his eyes.
**SMASH TO BLACK.** In the silence, the soft electric whine of an old television switching on.
*Why:* the audience gets the question in 3 seconds (*how did he end up here?*) and the whole film answers it.
*Production:* cut in the edit from Scene 4 footage. Costs no extra generation.

## SCENE 1 · "The Passenger" · Miami, 1986 · night · ~10 s
**Beat:** a scared kid learns from his first hero that fear is something you can drive through.
1. A boy's face, ten years old, lit blue by a television, a game controller in his small hands. The glow on his face
   turns pink and cyan, like neon, and the hum becomes the wind.
2. **Match cut:** the same small hands now grip a leather seat. He's in the passenger seat of a white 1980s sports
   coupe, feet not reaching the floor. Tommy drives one-handed, elbow out the window, unbothered.
3. **The hit:** a police cruiser slams alongside, its door 40 cm from the boy's window. Red-blue light explodes across
   his face, siren in his ear. He freezes.
4. Tommy doesn't look scared. He pulls a key ring with a **red die** from his shirt pocket, hangs it on the mirror, taps
   it once and looks at the boy: *for luck.* The boy swallows and nods.
5. Tommy brakes hard. The cruiser overshoots, spins on the wet asphalt and stops nose-first in the palm median.
6. **Mirror shot:** the boy's eyes in the mirror, laughing now. The die swings. Behind, two more sets of lights turn
   onto the boulevard.
7. The coupe dives into a tunnel. Black.

**Camera:** inside the car, handheld, close and claustrophobic. One overhead drone shot for the spin.
**Sound:** TV hum → wind → siren blast → muffled engine → the boy's laugh → tunnel echo → silence.

---

## SCENE 2 · "The Lesson" · Los Angeles, 1992 · golden hour · ~10 s
**Beat:** a teenager wants to be them, and Tommy shows him what that costs.
1. Out of the tunnel into blinding orange sunset. CJ's green lowrider rolls slow and low through a street of
   bungalows and palms. Tommy's white coupe follows.
2. **Mirror shot:** the eyes are fifteen now, under a backwards cap, from the back seat. The die hangs from CJ's mirror.
   Two cruisers are behind.
3. The teen leans forward between the seats, copying the way CJ steers. CJ catches him in the mirror, holds the look
   a second, and almost smiles.
4. **First sacrifice (the seed):** behind them, Tommy swings his coupe sideways across the street. The two cruisers
   brake hard and stop. Tommy, leaning on his car, gives a **two-finger salute** to the teen in the rear window.
   The teen doesn't understand yet.
5. CJ hits the hydraulics and the lowrider's nose leaps into the air. **Match cut on the jump.**

**Camera:** slower, wider and warmer. The longest takes in the film: this is the nostalgia.
**Sound:** heavy idle of the lowrider, kids on bikes, a dog, then brakes and the hiss of hydraulics.

---

## SCENE 3 · "The Wheel" · Los Angeles today · midday · ~10 s
**Beat:** he's the one driving now, and his heroes follow *him*.
1. The jump lands: a matte-black modern coupe drops down the cliffside ramp onto the Pacific Coast Highway, hard
   sun, heat shimmer.
2. **Mirror shot:** twenty-two, sunglasses, sweat at the temple, **his hands on the wheel** for the first time. The die
   hangs from his own mirror. A helicopter's shadow sweeps the car.
3. Freeway. Four cruisers close in. From the on-ramps, one by one: **Michael's** grey sedan slides calmly in front of a
   cruiser and makes it brake; **Franklin** cuts between two cruisers on the green bike; **Trevor's** red truck, the
   teddy bear on the push bar, shoves a cruiser into the dirt shoulder while Trevor laughs.
4. Egli looks in the mirror: his heroes are in a line *behind him*. A beat of realisation. He's leading.
5. His phone on the dashboard lights up: a live stream from a bridge at night, police lights waiting. Push in until the
   screen becomes the frame.

**Camera:** long lenses, highway compression, broadcast-style helicopter shots. Faster cutting.
**Sound:** rotor, engines, the crunch of the shove, then the phone's tinny live-stream audio taking over.

---

## SCENE 4 · "Wanted" · Florida today · night · ~10 s
**Beat:** the whole world is watching, and there's nowhere left to go.
1. Drone shot: the purple convertible leads the convoy (white coupe, green lowrider, grey sedan, green bike, red truck)
   over a long arched bridge, the neon skyline ahead, a river of red-blue behind.
2. **Mirror shot:** today's Egli, phone glow on his face. In the mirror, every hero, and behind them, every light.
3. On the bridge's walkway, teenagers film with phones held high and cheer as the convoy roars past.
4. At the top of the arch: a wall of cruisers across the road. He brakes and slides sideways. Behind, a second wall closes.
   The convoy stops. Trapped.
5. A helicopter spotlight finds his face. **(This is the cold-open moment.)** Then the light cuts out. Darkness.
   Out of it, a new sound: a huge propeller roaring up from the water.

**Camera:** spectacle wide, then tight and still on his face. The film's first stillness.
**Sound:** everything at maximum, then cut to almost nothing: insects, breath, the propeller rising.

---

## SCENE 5 · "One Seat" · same night · ~10 s
**Beat:** the old generation chooses to stay.
1. An airboat bursts out of the dark mangroves under the bridge, spray in the floodlights. **Lucia** at the helm,
   **Jason** standing, reaching up to the railing. There's room for one.
2. The old heroes look at the boat, then at each other. No words. Tommy nods once.
3. One by one they turn their cars sideways, the move from '92, now by everyone, into a single wall between the police
   and Egli. They step out and stand in front of it, backs to him, facing the lights.
4. Egli takes a step toward them. Tommy turns, puts a hand flat on his chest and stops him. He presses the key ring with the
   red die into his palm and closes his fingers over it.
5. Egli climbs the railing and drops into the airboat. Jason catches him by the arm.

**Camera:** handheld, intimate, eye level. Faces at arm's length.
**Sound:** the propeller idling, sirens muffled as if underwater, one held breath.

---

## SCENE 6 · "First Light" · dawn · ~10 s
**Beat:** gratitude, and the road ahead.
1. The airboat cuts through the bay as the sky turns gold. Egli looks back.
2. On the bridge, small now, his heroes stand in a ring of red-blue light with their hands raised. Tommy lifts **two
   fingers to his brow.** Egli's throat moves.
3. He opens his hand: the red die catches the first ray of sun.
4. Lucia glances at him and slides her sunglasses on. Jason gives him a nod. Egli turns forward.
5. Drone rises straight up: the airboat carving a white V through a golden bay, the bridge and its lights shrinking
   behind. It keeps rising. Title space in the sky.

**Text in the edit:** `1986 · 1992 · 2013 · 2026` → `They brought me here.`

## To decide together before the prompts
1. **The cold open from the bridge:** yes or no?
2. **One line of dialogue:** a single line in Scene 5 can land harder than any shot, e.g. Tommy: *"Go. It's your game now."*
   Or we keep the whole film silent.
3. **Weapons in Scene 5:** in the standoff the heroes could hold pistols low at their sides, facing the police wall
   (version A), with no firing. Or empty hands (version B).
4. **Egli resisting** (step 4 of Scene 5): it adds the emotional peak but takes ~1.5 s from elsewhere. Keep it?

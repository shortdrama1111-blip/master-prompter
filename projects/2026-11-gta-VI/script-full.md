# THEY BROUGHT ME HERE: Full Script & Production Bible (v3)

**Runtime:** ~76 s · **Format:** vertical 9:16 · **Model:** Seedance 2.5 · **Music:** added in the edit
**Genre/tone:** grounded crime drama. Real streets, real sweat, real weight. Quiet before the storm, silence before the hit.
**Status:** Scene 1 is in production (v3 + middle fix). Scenes 2–6 are a **draft for discussion**, one scene at a time, before any prompt.

---

## PART 1: THE STORY

**Logline.** A boy who grew up inside these worlds is raised, era by era, by the men who drove them. Every era the police
get closer and his heroes take more of the weight. On the last night, trapped on a bridge, the new generation comes to
take him. There is one seat. The old ones stay behind.

**Theme.** Growing up means your heroes let you go.
**Emotional line.** wonder → courage → responsibility → loss → gratitude.

**The wanted level (escalation across the film)**
| Era | Police | Heroes with Egli |
|---|---|---|
| 1986 | 1 cruiser → radio call → 3 cruisers | Tommy |
| 1992 | 2 cruisers | CJ, Tommy |
| Today LA | 4 cruisers + helicopter | Michael, Franklin, Trevor (+ Tommy, CJ in the line) |
| Today Florida | two roadblocks, officers with drawn pistols, helicopter, crowd filming | everyone |
| Finale | the full force | Lucia & Jason come for him |

**Motifs**
| Motif | Where it appears | Payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Rear-view mirror | Each era opens on Egli's eyes in a mirror: 10 → 15 → 22 → today | A life in four identical frames |
| The red die key ring | Tommy hangs it "for luck" (S1); it hangs in every car Egli rides | Tommy presses it into his palm (S5); it catches the sunrise (S6) |
| A car turned sideways | Tommy blocks the police alone (S2) | Every hero does it together (S5) |
| Two fingers to the brow | Tommy salutes the teen (S2) | Tommy's last image (S6) |
| The seat | passenger → back seat → driver → convoy leader → passenger of the new generation | Growing up told through where he sits |

## PART 3: THE SCRIPT

Legend: **CAM** camera · **ACT** action · **SND** sound (music comes in the edit) · **DLG** dialogue · **TR** transition out · **WHY** the director's reason.

### COLD OPEN · "The Bridge" · 0:00–0:03 · cut from Scene 4 in the edit
- **CAM** extreme close-up on his hands on the steering wheel, then a 0.5 s tilt up to the face.
- **ACT** White knuckles on the wheel, red-blue strobe across them; the red die swings hard from the mirror above. Today's Egli, a police spotlight on his face, a wall of sirens. He closes his eyes.
- **SND** sirens at maximum → hard cut to silence → the faint whine of an old TV switching on.
- **TR** smash to black.
- **WHY** The audience gets the question in 3 seconds: *how did he end up here?* The film answers it.

## PART 4: PRODUCTION NOTES
- **One scene at a time:** treatment → discussion → prompt → generation → review.
- **Every scene has one conflict event**, and every transition is motivated (match cut, whip, sound bridge, light, black frame).
- **Hero voices:** recorded or designed separately and laid in during the edit; hero lines are staged in profile or off-mirror to hide lip-sync. Police and extras are voiced by Seedance.
- **Never a freeze-frame.** Title space is made with movement (the rising drone).
- **Reference order per scene:** characters → location → vehicle → props, only what is in that scene.
